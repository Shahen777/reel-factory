# Hooks, retention, emotions, endings

A working cheat sheet for short vertical video. Numbers in industry articles are rarely backed by primary data,
so this lists techniques only. Your own analytics (3-second views, retention curve, shares) are the judge.

## How to use it

- **Pick, do not pile up.** One video = 1 hook, 1–2 retention tricks, 1 emotion, 1 ending.
- **Rotate.** Do not use the same hook type twice in a row. Note what you used.
- **Be honest.** A hook promises only what the video shows. No invented facts or health promises.
- **Three layers in the first 1–2 seconds:** moving picture, voice or sound, short text of 3–7 words.
  It must work with the sound off.

## 20 hook types

| # | Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Against common belief | Everyone does X. Wrong. | "Everyone stretches before a run. Don't." |
| 2 | Prohibition | Never do X after Y | "Never wash a new dress like this" |
| 3 | The mistake | The main mistake in X | "The main mistake in a first date outfit" |
| 4 | Real experience | N years in X, here is what I learned | Only with the real experience of the person |
| 5 | Secret place | There is something here nobody knows | "This mall has a floor most people never see" |
| 6 | Result first | Start with the finale, explain after | First frame: the finished look |
| 7 | Mid-action | Open where something is already happening | The ball is already flying |
| 8 | Versus | X vs Y | "One dress, two styles: which one?" |
| 9 | Exact number | A precise figure and a promise | "3 minutes to a full look", only if measured |
| 10 | Experiment | I did X to find out Y | "I wore only black for a week" |
| 11 | List | N things that… | "3 things stylists never wear" |
| 12 | Confession | I did X wrong for years | "I packed my gym bag wrong for years" |
| 13 | Recognition | If you are X, this is about you | "If you always run late, watch this" |
| 14 | Challenge question | A question people want to answer | "What do you eat after training?" |
| 15 | Debate | A claim people want to argue with | "Sneakers go with everything" |
| 16 | Before / after | Two states side by side | Messy room, then the clean one |
| 17 | Countdown | Make it in N seconds | "15 seconds to change the whole look" |
| 18 | Dialogue | A line from the middle of a conversation | "— Where are we going? — Up." |
| 19 | Broken expectation | One start, a different twist | Epic walk towards a… vending machine |
| 20 | Sound hook | A sharp recognisable sound before words | Sizzle on a black frame |

## What kills the opening

Greetings, a logo first, the brand as the first word, a vague "you won't believe this", asking to subscribe
before any value, slow build-up, a hook longer than 3 seconds, a promise the video does not keep.

## Retention

1. **Open loop:** promise a specific answer at the start and give it at the end. Always close the loop.
2. **New picture every 1–3 seconds:** shot size, angle, speed.
3. **Mid-video re-hook:** "but that's not the main thing", a new sound, a hard change.
4. **Speed up towards the payoff:** shorter and shorter cuts, then a pause before the finale.
5. **Speed ramps:** fast, then a sudden slow-down on what matters.
6. **Sound drives the edit:** cuts on the beat, whooshes on moves, an impact on the payoff.
7. **No dead air in the voice:** pauses are short and deliberate.
8. **Text changes with the thought:** one layer, at least 1 second, never on a face.

## Emotions (pick one)

Surprise ("wait, that's possible?"), laughter (a relatable situation), awe and beauty (scale, light, motion),
appetite and physical pleasure (sizzle, steam, first bite), pride and "this is me" (worth sending to a friend),
light debate (something to write in the comments).

## Endings

1. **Answer to the hook:** the last line closes the first one.
2. **Loop:** the last frame and sound flow into the first, people rewatch.
3. **Last-second twist:** a small joke after the payoff.
4. **Silent finale:** the voice stops, one second of face or detail with one sound.
5. **Short punchline** people want to repeat.
6. **Look into the camera:** a smile or nod instead of a call to action.
7. **Reason to share:** "send this to someone who…". Not in every video.
8. **Cut at the peak:** end before the viewer gets tired.
