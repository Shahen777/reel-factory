# reel-hooks — hook, script, ending

Read `docs/HOOKS.md` (20 hook types, retention tricks, emotions, endings). Produce `SCRIPT.md` in the project folder.

## Per video pick exactly

- **1 hook** (first 1–2 s), working in three layers at once: moving picture, voice or sound, 3–7 words of text.
  It must work with the sound off.
- **1–2 retention tricks** (open loop, new picture every 1–3 s, mid-video re-hook, speed-up before the payoff).
- **1 emotion** (surprise, laughter, awe, appetite, pride/"this is me", light debate).
- **1 ending** (answer to the hook, loop to the first frame, last-second twist, silent finale, short punchline).

Rotate: do not use the same hook type in two videos in a row. Write which ones you used at the top of SCRIPT.md.

## SCRIPT.md format

```
HOOK (type): on-screen text / voice / picture
BEATS: 0–2 s … | 2–6 s … | … | last 2 s …
ON-SCREEN TEXT: one layer at a time, ≥1 s each, never on faces
VOICE-OVER: (if any) short sentences
ENDING (type): …
CAPTION: first line = second hook, under ~125 characters before "more"
HASHTAGS: 3–5 specific ones
```

## Honesty

The hook only promises what the video really shows. No invented facts, no fake numbers, no medical promises.
Avoid "you won't believe", greetings, logo-first openings and "subscribe" before any value.
